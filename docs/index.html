Why I started here

I was exploring better arrival information for trotros and school transport in Ghana. Before building a tracking app, I wanted to test the prediction itself.

This first experiment uses public bus journeys from Brazil. It tests minutes to a recorded endpoint. A Ghana pilot is still needed.

Lower errors are better. A higher two-minute percentage is better.

What improved

Adding past movement history brought Plus mean error down from 5.88 to 4.67 minutes on the same test journeys. The updated random forest reached 5.50 minutes.

What this does not settle

The speed baseline gets more estimates within two minutes. Random forest had lower validation error than Plus. The ranking changes across splits, and 11 test journeys are a small sample.

04 / Methods & limits

What went into the prediction?

The experiment is reproducible. Both result exports include the predictions behind the tables.

The task and the inputs

Predict minutes until the final recorded GPS point. Inputs include current position, a known destination, elapsed time, recent speed, past distance traveled, stops, and time of day. Full-trip duration, speed and ratings are excluded. A recorded endpoint may not be a bus terminal.

Training, validation and testing

All observations from a journey stay in one partition. The 30 training journeys were divided into 22 fitting journeys and eight validation journeys for choosing prediction settings. Models were then refitted on all 30 training journeys and evaluated on the 11 test journeys. Learned models use the same movement-history features.

The original test results had already been inspected before this experiment. The rerun is exploratory. New journeys and repeated trip-level evaluation are needed before making a general performance claim.

What a transport app would still need

Live location, route destinations, and new local journey data would be needed to test this in Ghana. School transport would also need separate boarding, pickup and safety systems. This prototype evaluates arrival prediction only.
